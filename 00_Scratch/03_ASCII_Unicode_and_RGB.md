## Upcoming Sections in This Notebook

1. **ASCII (`29:54`):** How computers map numbers to letters (`A = 65 = 01000001`, `HI! = 72 73 33`).
2. **Unicode & Emojis:** Expanding from 8 bits to 16/24/32 bits (`UTF-8`) to represent every human alphabet and emoji (including skin-tone modifiers and Zero-Width Joiners).
